#Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard – Power BI

#Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI. The objective of the dashboard is to conduct a comprehensive analysis of Blinkit's sales performance, customer satisfaction, and inventory distribution.

The analysis uses key performance indicators (KPIs), interactive visualizations, and multiple business dimensions to identify sales trends, evaluate outlet performance, understand customer preferences, and uncover potential opportunities for business optimization.
The dashboard enables stakeholders to analyze performance across fat content, item type, outlet characteristics, outlet size, outlet location, and outlet type.

Business Objectives:-The primary objectives of this project are to:

Analyze overall sales performance using key business KPIs.
Evaluate the impact of product fat content on sales and customer ratings.
Identify high- and low-performing item categories.
Compare sales performance across different outlet types and locations.
Understand the relationship between outlet size and sales contribution.
Analyze the influence of outlet establishment on overall sales.
Evaluate customer satisfaction using average product ratings.
Provide an interactive dashboard for data-driven business analysis.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes the following primary KPIs:

| KPI                 | Description                                                |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Total Sales**     | Overall revenue generated from all items sold.             |
| **Average Sales**   | Average revenue generated per sale/item transaction.       |
| **Number of Items** | Total number of items sold.                                |
| **Average Rating**  | Average customer rating associated with the products sold. |

These KPIs provide a high-level overview of Blinkit's overall business performance while allowing users to analyze the metrics across multiple dimensions.

Dashboard Analysis

 1. Total Sales by Fat Content

This analysis evaluates the relationship between **product fat content and total sales**.

The dashboard compares sales performance across different fat-content categories and provides additional KPI analysis, including:
 Average Sales
 Number of Items
 Average Rating

This view helps identify differences in customer demand and sales contribution across product fat-content segments.
2. Total Sales by Item Type
This section analyzes sales performance across different **item types/categories**.
The analysis helps identify:

 High-performing product categories
 Low-performing product categories
 Differences in average sales
 Item volume across categories
 Customer rating patterns

The comparison provides a clearer understanding of which product categories contribute most significantly to overall revenue.
 3. Fat Content by Outlet for Total Sales
This analysis examines **total sales by outlet while segmenting products according to fat content**.
The dashboard provides a comparative view of:

Total Sales
Average Sales
Number of Items
Average Rating

This enables analysis of whether different outlet locations or outlet formats demonstrate different sales patterns based on product fat content.

4. Total Sales by Outlet Establishment
This visualization evaluates how **outlet establishment characteristics and establishment period** relate to sales performance.
The analysis can be used to identify changes in sales contribution across outlets based on their establishment timeline and understand how outlet maturity relates to revenue generation.

5. Percentage of Sales by Outlet Size
This analysis examines the contribution of different **outlet sizes** to overall sales.
The dashboard presents the percentage distribution of total sales across outlet-size categories, helping identify how outlet capacity and size are associated with revenue contribution.
6. Sales by Outlet Location

This section evaluates the **geographic distribution of sales** across different outlet locations.
The analysis enables users to compare sales performance across location tiers and identify geographic differences in customer demand and revenue generation.
7. All Metrics by Outlet Type

This section provides a comprehensive comparison of all major KPIs across different **outlet types**.
The following metrics are analyzed:

Total Sales
Average Sales
Number of Items
Average Rating

This provides an overall performance view of different outlet formats and allows users to compare operational and customer-related metrics in a single view.
